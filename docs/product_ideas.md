# Memories Wall
The Memories Wall is a high-fidelity, interactive digital workspace designed for personal reflection and community sharing. It combines a tactile, physical aesthetic with modern interactive features:

- Interactive 3D Environment: The central workspace features draggable cards (Sticky Notes and Polaroids) that use natural physics, deep 3D shadows, and realistic pins to create a sense of physical space.
- Tactile Organization: Memories are color-coded via category pins (Health, Kindness, Family, etc.), making the board easy to scan visually.
- Information Density: Cards are scaled down by 30% to maximize screen real estate, allowing for a rich, "lived-in" wall of content.
- Dynamic Utilities:
  - Collapsible Sidebar: A sleek navigation menu for filtering by category or viewing "My Memories."
  - Contextual Details Panel: A sliding panel that reveals specific memory details, including a collapsible Comments section for community interaction.
  - Footer Controls: Includes a "Recently Added" feed and a "Start a Memory" creator, both accessible via a unified collapsible footer layout.
  - Snap to Grid: A toggleable utility for users who prefer structured alignment over a natural scattered look.
